# Auto Decorator

Select a structure in the editor, tap **Deco**, pick a template. The mod decorates it for you, a little differently every time. Everything runs offline.

## How to use
1. Build your structure with normal blocks and **select it** (spikes, orbs and portals can be in the selection. They're never touched).
2. Tap **Deco** (top right of the editor).
3. Pick a template and tap **Use**.
4. A preview appears with three buttons:
   - **Shuffle** gives you a new variation.
   - **Apply** keeps the decoration. The **Undo Deco** button removes all of it in one tap.
   - **Cancel** removes the preview.

## Scenes
At the bottom of the list there are 6 **Scenes**. They don't decorate blocks: they fill the screen (or the area you selected) with a full background look, like a cutscene. Everything goes behind gameplay.

## Options in the template list
- **Density**: Light, Medium or Heavy. Heavy has no object limit.
- **Gradient**: colours fade across the structure.
- **Mirror**: symmetric decoration.
- **Animate**: gears spin. Only those objects get a group ID, with a single rotate trigger.
- **Keep my blocks**: don't cover your blocks with the template's fill.
- **Random**: picks a template for you.
- **Match nearby**: reuses the template and look of the closest structure you already decorated.
- **Save as template**: decorate a small structure by hand, select the blocks *and* their decoration, then save. The mod learns what goes on tops, sides, corners and inside.
- **Star**: marks a favourite. Favourites go to the top of the list.

Only decoration objects are placed, so your gameplay never changes.
